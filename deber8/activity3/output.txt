--- Complexity Summary ---
ComparisonCountingSort : O(n^2) comparisons, O(n) extra space
DistributionCountingSort: O(n+k) time,       O(n+k) extra space
(k = range of values)

Differences:
  1. Time: DistributionCounting is vastly faster for large n.
  2. Input: Distribution requires integer keys in a bounded range.
  3. Stability: DistributionCounting is stable; ComparisonCounting
     as implemented here may not handle duplicates correctly.
  4. Space: Distribution needs O(k) extra for the frequency table.
