=== Swap Without Extra Storage ===

Method 1 (XOR swap):
  Before: u = 42, v = 99
  After : u = 99, v = 42

Method 2 (Arithmetic add/sub swap):
  Before: u = 1000, v = -250
  After : u = -250, v = 1000

Inline XOR swap (no function):
  Before: u = 7, v = 3
  After : u = 3, v = 7

Explanation:
  XOR swap relies on the property: a ^ a = 0, a ^ 0 = a
  Step 1: u = u ^ v
  Step 2: v = u ^ v = (u^v)^v = u
  Step 3: u = u ^ v = (u^v)^u = v
  No temporary variable is needed at any step.
